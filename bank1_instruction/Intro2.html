Decisions

You will make your decision over two periods.

In Period 1, both you and the other participant in the bank simultaneously choose whether to withdraw or stay.

  • If you withdraw, you immediately take out your 10 points (entire deposits) and make no further decisions.
  • If you stay, your deposit remains in the bank and you move to Period 2.

In Period 2, only participants who chose to stay in Period 1 continue in the game and will again choose whether to withdraw or stay.

After both participants have made decisions, you will see how many points you received, along with the other's decision.

On the next pages, you will see the game screen and payoff information for each state (Low, Medium, and High).
